# MAINXP — PRODUCT NORTH (first-principles directive)

The founder's first-principles brief, triaged honestly. This file is the
referee for every future wave: a feature that doesn't strengthen the loop
below gets removed or demoted, never "kept because it exists".

## The one reason

**Help a person become the person capable of creating the life they want.**

The loop: who I want to become → what life I want → what season I'm in →
what goals matter → what mission moves the goal → what ONE result matters
today → what action I take → what distracts me → how I recover → what I
learn → what my behavior proves → who I am becoming.

## The promise (the product at maturity)

"You said you wanted X. Your behavior this month shows Y. This is the
pattern stopping you. This is the one change most likely to move you
forward. Do this next." — a decision-and-behavior engine, not a tracker.

## The three levels (every feature must belong to one)

- **NOW** — Main Quest · Arena · non-négociables · Reset · Minimum Day
- **DIRECTION** — Season · goals · missions · milestones · identity
- **LEARNING** — Coach · patterns · weekly review · progress · evidence

One question per screen: Home *what matters now?* · Arena *what am I
focusing on?* · Coach *what am I missing?* · Progress *what is changing?*
· Moi *who am I becoming?* · Dojo *what am I training?* · Cercle *who
keeps me accountable?*

## Already true (do NOT rebuild; defend)

Surprise XP, no advertised amounts · no-shame states everywhere · Cercle
without feed/followers/counts · Minimum Day · Comeback Quest · rest mode
(+ flame bridging) · coach citing real numbers with confidence discipline ·
event system-of-record · anti-farming (caps, diminishing, one gratitude
payout/day) · original Block Hero + icon family (asset policy) · French,
tutoiement, no hype.

## Standing rules (apply to every wave)

- The interface is simple; the intelligence is complex. Reduce decisions.
- ONE filled button per screen. If four purple buttons are visible, the
  hierarchy is wrong.
- Whitespace over containers; cards only for interactive groups, insights,
  hero moments. Radius scale: controls 10–12 · surfaces 16 · hero 22+.
  Shadows near-invisible.
- Cut copy ~40%: confident, calm, precise; poetry rarely so it stays
  powerful. Never ask what the app already knows.
- Numbers are the storytelling: big, tabular, few.
- XP is reinforcement, never the product; every XP event can explain
  itself.
- Notifications: few, factual, never streak-panic. Retention comes from
  ACTION → INSIGHT → IDENTITY → BETTER ACTION.
- Every screen passes: one question, one action, what can go, what can AI
  infer, would a top App Store product ship this?
